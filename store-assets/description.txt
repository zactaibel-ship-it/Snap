=======================================

Snip turns any cooking video into a recipe you can actually use.

Paste a link from YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram — Snip's AI extracts a complete recipe in seconds. Ingredients, quantities, steps, cook time. Everything.

SAVE FROM ANYWHERE
Save recipes from your favourite cooking videos. YouTube tutorials, TikTok viral dishes, Instagram Reels — Snip handles them all.

FOLLOW YOUR FAVOURITE CREATORS
Subscribe to cooking channels and creators. When they post a new video, Snip automatically saves the recipe to your library. Never miss a dish again.

PLAN YOUR WEEK
Drag recipes into a weekly meal planner. Mix and match breakfast, lunch, and dinner across the week.

SHOP WITH ONE TAP
Snip builds your shopping list automatically — ingredients merged, duplicates removed, sorted by aisle. Tap to shop on Tesco or Sainsbury's.

COOK WITH CONFIDENCE
Cook Mode gives you full-screen, step-by-step instructions with the screen always on. Tap through at your own pace.

Start free. Upgrade to Snip Pro for unlimited extractions and creator follows.
